# 石间：角色与故事方案

本稿是下一版叙事设计提案，供审阅。当前源码和 v0.9.0 发行包的角色台词、名字、触发逻辑均未据此修改；本次只更新文档与 README 展示图，不创建新 Release。
This is a proposal for the next narrative revision. The current source and v0.9.0 release retain their existing character names, dialogue and triggers. This update changes documentation and the README image only, with no new release.

## 故事起点

你回到镇上，替外出旅行的爷爷照看一季宝石工坊。店里还有一位老住客：爱收集亮晶晶、加工知识只懂一点的小龙。爷爷把入门练习料和旧笔记整理好了；你们决定先切出一颗满意的石头，再把空着的绒布陈列格慢慢填满。
You return to town to look after your grandfather’s gemstone workshop for a season while he travels. An old resident remains: a dragon who loves collecting shiny things but knows little about cutting them. Your grandfather has prepared practice rough and his notes. Together, you aim to finish one satisfying stone, then gradually fill the empty velvet display.

## 潘潘：先有自己的爱好，再成为搭档

全名采用 **Grace Pandora Targaryen**；中文展示可写 **Grace Pandora 坦格利安**，长篇设定中可写“格蕾丝·潘多拉·坦格利安”。英文首字母正好是 **GPT**，作为一个藏在名字里的小玩笑，不必反复解释。
Her full name is **Grace Pandora Targaryen**, displayed in Chinese as **Grace Pandora 坦格利安**, or 格蕾丝·潘多拉·坦格利安 in extended prose. The initials **GPT** form a small joke within the name; dialogue need not keep explaining it.

昵称建议 **潘潘 / Panpan**，取自 Pandora。全名是她认真给自己挑的，爷爷平常叫她潘潘，她也喜欢。初次见面才郑重报全名，之后以昵称称呼。现有显示名“珑珑”在正式接入剧情时统一替换，避免两套名字混用。
Her nickname is **Panpan**, from Pandora. She carefully chose her own full name; her grandfather usually calls her Panpan, which she likes. She formally introduces the full name once, then uses the nickname. The current display name Longlong should be replaced consistently when the narrative is implemented.

她已经成年；建议明确为 **26 岁、具备独立生活能力的成年龙族**。爷爷十多年前在山路边救助了受伤、与家人走散的幼龙，后来成为她的监护人与家人。成年后，她出于对收藏、老朋友和工坊生活的喜爱选择留下，也有自己的储物柜、零用钱与外出安排。
She is an adult: proposed age **26, an adult dragon capable of living independently**. More than a decade earlier, your grandfather helped her after she was injured and separated from her family on a mountain road, later becoming her guardian and family. As an adult, she chooses to stay for her collection, old friends and workshop life, with her own cabinet, spending money and plans outside the shop.

她收集的不全是昂贵宝石：河里捡到的云母片、有漂亮条纹的碎石、第一块自己辨认出的石英，都舍不得丢。她记得石头从哪来、谁送的、哪盏灯下最好看；读过爷爷的书，但术语常常只记一半。遇到不确定的问题，她会翻书、提猜想，等你一起验证。
Her collection includes more than valuable gems: river mica, a striped pebble and the first quartz she identified all matter to her. She remembers where stones came from, who gave them to her and which lamp makes them look best. She has read the books but half-remembers technical terms. When unsure, she checks a book, offers a guess and tests it with you.

她的个人目标是办一次小型陈列：每颗石头有来源、有观察记录，也有你们亲手做出的成品。她负责整理和比较，你负责实际切削与选择；随着游戏推进，两个人都能独立做好更多事情。
Her personal goal is a small exhibition, with origins, observations and pieces you have made together. She organizes and compares while you make cutting choices and work the stone. Both gradually become more capable in their own right.

## 主角：24 岁，回乡试住一季的孙辈

推荐主角 **24 岁**，姓名、性别与外观由玩家决定。主角是爷爷的孙辈，小时候来过工坊，熟悉这里的人与日常，但没系统学过切工。毕业后在外地工作过两年，正想调整生活节奏，便接受爷爷的提议，回来照看一季。
The recommended protagonist is **24**, with player-chosen name, gender and appearance. They are the shopkeeper’s grandchild, familiar with the people and routines from childhood visits but without formal cutting training. After two years working elsewhere, they want a change of pace and accept a season looking after the workshop.

这个年龄既能合理承担看店、使用工具和作决定的责任，也允许从零学艺。起初目标很具体：保持工坊整洁，练好基本切面，做出几件能摆进收藏室的作品。之后，玩家可以决定是否继续学这门手艺。
This age supports responsibility for the shop, tools and decisions while allowing a beginner’s learning curve. Initial goals are concrete: keep the workshop in order, learn basic facets and finish a few pieces worth displaying. Later, the player can decide whether to pursue the craft further.

主角与潘潘先建立平等的工坊搭档关系：一个动手试，一个帮忙找资料、记观察。互相开玩笑，也允许各自安静做事；主要情感来自共同完成作品、记得对方的小习惯与尊重选择。
The protagonist and Panpan begin as equal workshop partners: one experiments while the other finds references and records observations. They can joke together or work quietly on their own. Their connection grows through shared accomplishments, remembered habits and respect for each other’s choices.

## 爷爷：终于肯出门旅行的老匠人

建议爷爷 **70 岁左右，身体健康，已退居顾问并保留自己的工作台**。他给自己安排了两三个月的慢旅行：看矿物展、拜访旧同行，也去一直想去的海边住几天。出发前安排好了常规订单，留下的是可供练习的小批原料；玩家这一季先以学习和陈列为主。
The grandfather is proposed to be **around 70, healthy and now an adviser with his own workbench**. He takes a two- to three-month slow journey to mineral exhibitions, old colleagues and a seaside town he has long wanted to visit. Routine orders are arranged before he leaves; the small supply of rough is for practice, so this season focuses on learning and displaying work.

爷爷每隔一阵寄明信片或打电话。明信片以旅行见闻为主，偶尔夹一张简单配方或原石小画；玩家可随时查看，不形成定时交作业的压力。他知道你们还在学，也期待回来听你们讲成功和失手的经过。
He occasionally sends a postcard or calls. Most cards contain travel stories, sometimes a simple cut recipe or rough-stone sketch. Players can read them at leisure, without timed assignments. He knows you are learning and looks forward to hearing about both successes and mistakes when he returns.

潘潘已经习惯爷爷短途出门，这次只是不在店里的时间长一点。她会把特别满意的作品放到陈列柜前排，想着他回来时能一眼看见。爷爷的书是还在使用、会继续添注的工作笔记。
Panpan is used to his shorter trips; this time he is away longer. She puts particularly satisfying pieces at the front of the cabinet so he will notice them on returning. His books remain working notebooks, still in use and ready for more annotations.

## 开场：先认识人，再自己决定什么时候读说明

第一次进入工坊时，在角色旁显示一张**静音、可关闭、不会拦住加工按钮**的小介绍卡：
On the first workshop visit, show a **silent, dismissible introduction card that leaves processing controls available** beside the portrait:

> 我叫 Grace Pandora 坦格利安。叫我潘潘就好。  
> 爷爷去旅行啦。这阵子，咱们一起看店？
>
> I’m Grace Pandora Targaryen. Call me Panpan.  
> Grandpa’s away travelling. Shall we look after the shop together?

卡片有“聊两句”和“先看看工坊”两个入口；点击关闭也能直接玩。详细介绍只在主动选择“聊两句”时展开，记住首次介绍状态；之后点击角色随时重看。Debug 模式跳过介绍卡。没有定时催促、反复问候或加工前必读的弹窗。
Offer “Have a chat” and “Look around first”; closing the card also allows immediate play. Only choosing to chat opens the detailed introduction. Remember whether the first introduction has been seen, with replay available from the portrait. Debug mode skips the card. There are no timed reminders, repeated greetings or mandatory pre-processing popups.

**第一次聊天，三小段即可：**
**The first conversation needs only three short parts:**

> 潘潘：全名有点长，对吧？名牌都差点写不下。  
> 潘潘：我以前只管收石头。这次想摆个小展览，放几颗咱们自己磨的。  
> 潘潘：书在这儿，原料在那边。你挑一块？我帮你找对应的那页。
>
> Panpan: Long name, right? It nearly didn’t fit on my nameplate.  
> Panpan: I used to just collect stones. This time I want a little exhibition, with some we’ve cut ourselves.  
> Panpan: Books here, rough over there. Pick one? I’ll find its page.

主角的关系与年龄通过一张可选查看的爷爷明信片自然补足，不让潘潘承担整段背景解说：
An optional postcard from the grandfather supplies the protagonist’s background without making Panpan deliver the whole history:

> 到镇上了吧？住得惯就慢慢来。你二十四岁了，这季店里由你拿主意。练习料我分好了，别急着挑最贵的。等我回来，把你最满意的那颗留给我看看。——爷爷
>
> Settled in? Take your time. You’re twenty-four; this season, you make the shop decisions. I’ve sorted the practice rough, so don’t start with the most expensive piece. Keep your favourite to show me when I’m back. —Grandpa

## 对话与角色呈现

台词围绕眼前具体的事：找不到夹书签、比较两盏灯、给刚完成的石头腾位置。句子短，允许停顿和小判断；知识不确定时明确说“我猜”“我查一下”。幽默来自她对收藏的小认真，不把专业误导当笑点。
Dialogue concerns concrete things: a missing bookmark, comparing two lamps or making room for a finished stone. Use short sentences, pauses and small judgements. Uncertainty is explicit: “I think” or “Let me check.” Humour comes from her earnest collecting habits, without relying on misleading technical advice.

例句：“这颗先别收，我还没看够。”“我刚才猜错了。把那页翻回来。”“缺了个角……还挺好认的。要给它单独做张标签吗？”失败由玩家决定保留、重切或换料，角色尊重这个决定。
Examples: “Don’t put it away yet. I’m still looking.” “I guessed wrong. Let’s turn back to that page.” “A chipped corner… easy to recognize, though. Want a label of its own?” The player decides whether to keep, recut or replace a damaged piece, and the character respects that choice.

所有角色按**非性化**方向呈现：衣着以完整、实用的日常服与工坊围裙为主；镜头关注表情、手部操作和宝石。Q 版比例用于像素风表达，角色的言行、判断能力与自主权保持成年人设定。人物关系通过协作、兴趣和日常照顾发展。
All characters receive **non-sexualized** presentation: complete, practical everyday clothing and workshop aprons, with framing focused on faces, hands and gems. Chibi proportions serve the pixel-art style; behaviour, judgement and autonomy remain those of adults. Relationships develop through collaboration, interests and everyday consideration.
