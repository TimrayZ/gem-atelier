# 石间小铺 v0.11.0 · 首发体验版

第一版范围收束为十种原材料、十种切工。游戏名为《石间小铺》，英文名 Gem Atelier；兼容原有存档目录。潘潘中文全名为格蕾丝·潘多拉·坦格利安，英文全名 Grace Pandora Targaryen。
The first-launch scope is ten materials and ten cuts. The game is named 石间小铺 / Gem Atelier and retains the existing save directory. Panpan’s full names are 格蕾丝·潘多拉·坦格利安 and Grace Pandora Targaryen.

材料：亚历山大变石、紫水晶、蓝宝石、钻石、透明正长石、合成莫桑石、祖母绿、粉色尖晶石、金色托帕石、红宝石。切工：八角单翻、椭圆、八重对称、长方阶梯、圆形明亮心箭试验、枕形、梨形、马眼、切角三角、六边阶梯。心箭试验仍未认证为八心八箭。
Materials: alexandrite, amethyst, sapphire, diamond, orthoclase, synthetic moissanite, emerald, pink spinel, golden topaz and ruby. Cuts: octagonal single, oval, eightfold, rectangular step, experimental round brilliant, cushion, pear, marquise, clipped triangle and hexagonal step. The round brilliant is not verified as hearts and arrows.

原石按矿物晶形建立凸实体，添加像素生长线、棱线与小蚀像。它们是简化晶形，不是完整晶簇或原子晶格模拟。合成莫桑石使用晶体片示例，未把罕见天然微晶冒充珠宝级大原料。
Rough uses simplified mineral-habit convex solids, with pixel growth lines, edges and etch motifs rather than full clusters or atomic lattices. Moissanite uses a synthetic crystal-piece example, distinct from rare tiny natural crystals.

像素通道去掉固定 RGB 调色板，采用 12 波段光路、光源光谱、拟合吸收和有限色阶。钻石跟随光源，莫桑石使用更高的 B–G 色散代表参数。真实通道保留 20 波段；两者都公开提供 6 / 12 / 20 次内部反射上限。高色散是否显示更多火彩仍依赖切工、角度和光源。
The pixel channel replaces fixed RGB palettes with 12-band transport, source spectra, fitted absorption and bounded colour steps. Diamond follows its source and moissanite uses a higher representative B–G dispersion. The detailed channel retains 20 bands; both expose 6/12/20 internal-reflection limits. Visible fire still depends on cut, view and illumination.

导入支持 GemCAD .asc 与 Gem Cut Studio .gcs 的常见平面刻面子集：角度、中心距、分度，GCS 也可用明确法线。会归一化尺寸并检查封闭实体；采用所选宝石 RI。平底底尖、磨砂、非零 ASC 分度偏移、缺少 depth 的 GCS 等当前会提示不支持。二进制 .gem 未实现。guide 面不加入成品。导入数据随收藏保存与重载。
Import supports common planar-facet subsets of GemCAD ASC and Gem Cut Studio GCS: angles, centre distances, indices and explicit GCS normals. It normalizes size, checks closure and uses the selected material’s RI. Flat culets, frosting, nonzero ASC gear offsets and GCS without depth are currently rejected with explanations. Binary GEM is not supported. Guide facets are excluded; imported recipes persist with collections.

内附两份等价的原创练习切工，CC0 授权。格式的公开说明不代表第三方设计都可再分发。本作独立实现解析器，没有打包下载所得的第三方切工库。
Two equivalent original practice designs are included under CC0. Public format documentation does not automatically grant redistribution rights to third-party designs. The parser is independently implemented; no downloaded third-party design collection is bundled.

潘潘默认安静，点击肖像可听拟声自我介绍、查看背景故事。主角 24 岁，潘潘 26 岁，爷爷健康且外出旅行；角色保持实用服装与非性化呈现。常规手记仍可主动打开，不阻塞加工。
Panpan is quiet by default; clicking her portrait opens a synthesized-voice introduction and story. The protagonist is 24, Panpan 26, and the healthy grandfather is travelling. Presentation remains practical and non-sexualized. Contextual notes remain optional and do not gate processing.

验证：全部 100 种材料／切工组合完成；原有 5296 项模型检查零失败；ASC/GCS 等价几何、导入加工、保存恢复通过；同切形三角度的彩色像素统计为钻石 8404、莫桑石 13472，属于回归样本而非普遍火彩倍率。像素钻石日光平均 RGB 约 (0.548,0.547,0.550)，暖光约 (0.587,0.477,0.356)，用于防止固定蓝色回归。
Validation: all 100 material/cut combinations complete; the existing 5296 model checks pass; ASC/GCS geometry equivalence, processing and save restoration pass. Across three views of one geometry, coloured-pixel counts are 8404 for diamond and 13472 for moissanite, a regression sample rather than a universal fire multiplier. Pixel-diamond mean RGB is approximately (0.548,0.547,0.550) under daylight and (0.587,0.477,0.356) under warm light, guarding against a fixed-blue regression.

光谱与强度为游戏拟合，双折射、偏振、真实夹杂分布和严格物理破裂仍不完整；火彩采样与反射上限可能造成细小闪烁。切工和温度参数不用于现实加工指导。
Spectra and strengths are game fits. Birefringence, polarization, true inclusion distributions and physical fracture remain incomplete; spectral sampling and bounce caps can produce fine shimmer. Cutting and heat parameters are not manufacturing guidance.
