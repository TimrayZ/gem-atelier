# 0.9.0 · Windows 体验版 / Windows preview

宝石光学层独立以 568×414 绘制，背景保留 284×207 像素画布。四点旋转分层采样使用原生像素范围，默认最多 12 次内部反射，可选 6/20 次。Cauchy 近似修正 B–G 与 F–C 色散波段混用。仍可能存在细小混叠。
The optical pass renders at 568×414 separately from the 284×207 background. Four rotated stratified samples cover native pixels, with a default 12-bounce limit and 6/20 alternatives. Cauchy dispersion now uses the B–G convention. Fine aliasing can remain.

百科与渲染共享拟合吸收参数；加入压力、简化晶向、真实截角损伤、损伤存档和继续完工。新增独立绒布收藏室，保存的真实边界用于缩略图和重新查看，保留重量与缺角；返回时恢复工坊进度。
The encyclopedia and renderer share fitted absorption data. Pressure and simplified crystal orientation affect real-volume chipping, persisted damage and continued finishing. A velvet collection room renders stored boundaries and reopens pieces with their weight and chips, preserving the active workshop session.

验证：25 种材料／切形组合完成；5296 项几何、流程、光学检查零失败；方向损伤、完工与保存、默认静音、单次抛光、快速加工、百科真实点击和背景不染色检查通过。RTX 3060 Laptop GPU 上，1120×720 窗口中 73 任务钻石、20 次反射上限，30 帧旋转约 1330 ms，只代表本机。
Validation: all 25 material/cut combinations complete; 5296 geometry/process/optics checks pass; directional damage, completion, saves, silence, single polishing, fast processing, encyclopedia clicks and material-independent backdrop checks pass. On the development RTX 3060 Laptop GPU, a 73-task diamond at a 20-bounce limit took about 1330 ms for 30 rotating frames at 1120×720; this is a local measurement only.

温度、风险、评分为游戏值。崩角为凸平面截角，尚非真实凹入贝壳断口；没有把“玻璃切心箭必崩”当事实。圆形明亮式没有验证八心八箭。珑珑保持默认安静，声音和台词风格仍可改进。
Temperature, risk and score are gameplay values. Chips are convex truncations rather than concave conchoidal fractures; glass is not assumed to inevitably chip in a hearts-and-arrows style. The round brilliant is not verified for hearts and arrows. Longlong remains quiet by default; voice and dialogue style remain open for improvement.
